# Five-Funnel Copy Deck

Revision: 2026-08-02-r1  
Status: draft — owner approval required before canonical copy is changed

This is the single review sheet for the five paid-traffic packages. Claims are
limited to what the current product records promise; prices and delivery terms
must be read back from the live catalog before approval. The three standard
offers remain editable in `src/content/offers/*.json`; Blueprint and App Idea
remain owned by their existing repositories.

## 1. Owned Funnel Builder

- Buyer: creators, consultants, agencies, and small businesses with a digital
  product, service, or workshop who want pages and customer data in accounts
  they control.
- Offer: complete landing-page, checkout, publishing, and rollback system;
  $49 USD, optional $19 Conversion Copy Swipe File bump, configured upsells.
- Ad primary text: “Stop rebuilding your offer page from scratch. Owned Funnel
  Builder gives you a reusable page, checkout, and release workflow that you
  can keep in your own accounts. Review the promise, price, and checkout before
  you publish.”
- Headline: `Own your funnel builder`.
- Description: `A reusable funnel system for agent-assisted launches.`
- CTA: `Get the complete builder for $49`.
- Landing first fold: `Never rent your funnel builder again.` Keep the existing
  audience, $49 price, 30-day guarantee, and “own the source” language.
- Checkout/fulfillment: use the existing secure checkout, email consent,
  `30-day guarantee`, and builder delivery records.
- Creative: screen capture of the real editor → preview → checkout → release
  gates. No invented results or testimonials.
- Canonical sources: `src/content/offers/owned-funnel-builder.json`,
  `src/content/funnels/owned-funnel-builder.json`.
