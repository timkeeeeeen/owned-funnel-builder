# Plain-English launch interview

Gather only missing decisions. Infer implementation details yourself.

## Offer

- What are you selling, and what does the buyer receive?
- Who should buy it? Who should not?
- What specific result should they expect?
- What is the price, guarantee, and delivery timing?

## Evidence

- What real examples, screenshots, demonstrations, customer quotes, or numbers may be used?
- Which claims must not be made?
- What must a buyer already know, own, or be comfortable using?

## Funnel

- What small extra item would feel obvious at checkout?
- What should the buyer be offered immediately after purchase?
- Is there a second next-step offer?
- What should happen after the final decision?

## Brand and launch

- What name, logo, colors, images, video, and tone already exist?
- Which domain and support email should be used?
- Which payment account should be connected: Dodo or Stripe? Do the required Resend, Cloudflare, and GitHub accounts already exist?

Offer simple recommendations when the user is unsure. Mark unresolved facts as unresolved; never replace them with plausible fiction.
