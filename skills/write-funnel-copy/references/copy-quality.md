# Copy quality context

## Useful persuasion jobs

Select only the jobs the offer needs:

- State one immediately understood transformation.
- Name the costly old way and the more useful new way.
- Make the product tangible with previews, examples, or a walkthrough.
- Explain why the mechanism produces the result.
- Show exactly what is included.
- Establish evidence appropriate to the product's maturity.
- Qualify the right buyer and release the wrong buyer.
- Answer price, time, effort, compatibility, trust, and delivery objections.
- Reduce risk with a real guarantee or precise expectations.
- Repeat the primary action after moments of increased conviction.

## Language tests

- Could a buyer repeat the promise after one reading?
- Does each paragraph add a new fact, distinction, or reason to act?
- Can every claim be traced to product truth or supplied evidence?
- Does the CTA say what happens next?
- Does the bump complement the purchase without implying it is required?
- Does each upsell stand on its own and remain easy to decline?

## Avoid

- “Revolutionary,” “game-changing,” and similar unsupported adjectives.
- Empty “unlock,” “supercharge,” and “level up” phrasing.
- Benefits with no object, mechanism, example, or time context.
- Fake precision, invented social proof, and countdown pressure.
- Long founder stories before the buyer understands the offer.
