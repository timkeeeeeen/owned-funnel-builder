# Write Funnel Copy

Treat the model as capable. Use principles and evidence, not canned formulas.

## Process

1. Read `PROJECT.md`, the target offer data, and [references/copy-quality.md](references/copy-quality.md).
2. Identify the traffic promise, buyer awareness, desired action, real proof, prohibited claims, and offer economics.
3. Decide the persuasion jobs the page must perform. Choose their order for this offer instead of filling a fixed template.
4. Draft a specific headline, concrete subheadline, clear CTA, tangible offer explanation, proof, qualification, objection handling, risk reversal, and close as needed.
5. Make the bump a small, immediate enhancement to the core purchase. Make each upsell a distinct next result, not a vague bundle of more things.
6. Keep price, delivery, guarantee, prerequisites, and CTA wording consistent across the page, checkout, upsells, completion page, and email.
7. Edit for spoken clarity. Remove filler, inflated adjectives, unsupported certainty, and generic AI phrasing.
8. Leave facts visibly unresolved when evidence is missing and ask one concise human question.

## Standards

- Lead with the outcome, then explain the mechanism in ordinary language.
- Use concrete nouns, short sentences, and meaningful section headings.
- Qualify and disqualify explicitly when prerequisites matter.
- Show what the buyer gets and what using it looks like.
- Preserve message match with the ad or referring promise.
- Never fabricate testimonials, logos, metrics, urgency, scarcity, or guarantees.
- Do not disguise technical limitations. Translate them into honest buyer expectations.
