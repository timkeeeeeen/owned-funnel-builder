# Client Funnel Delivery Toolkit

## Client intake

Collect these facts in one document:

1. Product name, price, and currency.
2. Specific buyer and the situation that makes them ready.
3. Promise and the mechanism that makes it credible.
4. Exact deliverables and delivery method.
5. Proof that may be published.
6. Requirements and disqualifiers.
7. Refund, privacy, support, and response policies.
8. Main offer, optional bump, and up to two upsells.
9. Domain, brand assets, payment account, email sender, and analytics ownership.

## Claims approval

Have the client mark every numerical claim, testimonial, logo, deadline, guarantee, comparison, and product capability as Approved, Revise, or Remove. Save the dated approval with the launch record.

## Account handoff

The client should own the GitHub organization, Cloudflare account, domain, Dodo business, and email domain. Invite the operator; do not collect passwords in chat or documents. Store keys only in provider secret stores.

## Duplication workflow

1. Create a new repository from the clean builder.
2. Run the new-offer workflow with the approved intake.
3. Review copy in the visual editor.
4. Connect Dodo in test mode and verify product names and totals.
5. Connect delivery and send a test.
6. Publish a preview, review all screen sizes, and capture evidence.
7. Obtain final claims approval.
8. Publish production and record the exact commit and URL.

## Change requests

Separate copy edits, design edits, offer changes, and payment changes. Payment and delivery changes always require a fresh sandbox purchase. Keep a dated record of what changed and who approved it.
