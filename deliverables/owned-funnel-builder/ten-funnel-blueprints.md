# Ten Funnel Blueprints

Give one blueprint to your agent with the true facts about your offer. The section order is a starting point, not a cage.

## 1. Digital guide or template

Promise → visible preview → old way versus new way → contents → who it is for → objections → price → FAQ → final CTA.

Good bump: quickstart checklist. Good upsell: example pack.

## 2. Workshop

Outcome → date and format → what attendees will build → agenda → instructor credibility → prerequisites → ticket → FAQ.

Good bump: workbook. Good upsell: recording plus implementation session.

## 3. Course recording

Transformation → sample lesson → module map → practical outputs → pace and access → qualification → price → FAQ.

Good bump: templates. Good upsell: office hours.

## 4. Paid diagnostic

Pain and cost → what gets inspected → deliverable example → process → who qualifies → boundaries → fee → booking step.

Good bump: data-prep service. Good upsell: implementation roadmap.

## 5. Productized service

Concrete deliverable → before/after process → scope → timeline → proof → qualification → fixed price → intake.

Good bump: rush option. Good upsell: monthly maintenance.

## 6. Consulting offer

Business problem → point of view → engagement outputs → working model → fit/no-fit → credibility → next paid step.

Good bump: preparation audit. Good upsell: execution support.

## 7. Presale

Future outcome → honest build status → what early buyers receive → milestones → risks → founder fit → founding price → refund terms.

Good bump: onboarding call. Good upsell: extended access or team seats.

## 8. Waitlist with paid priority

Upcoming product → problem → product concept → early access benefits → timeline → free versus paid priority → clear terms.

Good bump: research pack. Good upsell: concierge setup.

## 9. Membership

Ongoing job → monthly cadence → current library → community or support → who participates well → cancellation → first month CTA.

Good bump: starter bundle. Good upsell: annual plan or private review.

## 10. Software starter kit

Build outcome → real product demo → architecture explained simply → included systems → requirements → supported path → license → price.

Good bump: prompt pack. Good upsell: app blueprints and production setup pack.

## Brief to paste into your agent

> Build blueprint [number] for [product]. Buyer: [who]. Promise: [result]. Price: [price]. Contents: [list]. Proof I can honestly use: [facts]. Requirements: [requirements]. Do not invent proof, urgency, or guarantees. Keep the checkout path obvious on desktop and mobile.
